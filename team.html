Meet the Team

The people behind MaxBuildsXJ and the builds, trails, and content.

Max

Founder / Builder / Driver

I’ve been around cars and Jeeps my whole life. Growing up in the garage with my family taught me everything from basic maintenance to full engine work. This site started as a way to share my builds, trail experiences, and everything I’ve learned working on Jeeps. My goal is to keep building rigs that can handle real terrain and help others get into off-roading.

Cayden Comstock

Trail Partner / Builder

Cayden is a key part of the MaxBuildsXJ crew. He helps on trail runs, builds, and recovery situations and is always involved in pushing rigs further and making trips more capable and fun. Whether it’s spotting on difficult lines or helping with fabrication work, he plays a big role in keeping everything moving forward.
